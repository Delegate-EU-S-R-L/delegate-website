# Delegate Website

Delegate is an AI-enabled business services company focused on helping small and medium-sized businesses improve their digital presence, operational workflows and internal efficiency through websites, automation and AI-assisted tools.

This repository contains the first public website/prototype for Delegate, built to present the company vision, services and early positioning.

## Project Goal

The goal of this project is to create a clear digital presence for Delegate and validate the positioning of a full-stack AI company for SMEs.

Delegate aims to combine business understanding, AI-assisted workflows and no-code/low-code execution to help companies move faster, reduce manual work and improve how they present and manage their services.

The website is designed to communicate:

- what Delegate does;
- which services it can offer to businesses;
- how websites, automations and AI workflows can create value;
- how traditional companies can use modern tools without building an internal tech team;
- how Delegate can evolve from service delivery into reusable AI-powered products.

## Company Vision

Delegate is being developed as a full-stack AI company for SMEs.

The vision is to support companies across different layers of digital transformation:

1. Digital presence through websites and landing pages;
2. Lead generation and contact flows;
3. Internal workflow automation;
4. AI-assisted document and communication processes;
5. Operational dashboards and structured data collection;
6. Vertical AI tools for specific business use cases;
7. Future matching systems and workflow agents.

The long-term objective is to move from single client projects to repeatable systems, reusable workflows and productized AI services.
